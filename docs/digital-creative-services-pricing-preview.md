This document records the customer-facing Preview pricing and the scope boundaries used by the Digital Seva catalogue and Pay Now centre.

| Package | Price | Included scope | Indicative timeline |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Basic | ₹5,999 | Up to 5 responsive pages; home/about/services/contact; WhatsApp and enquiry form; basic SEO, sitemap and Search Console setup/submission when domain access is provided | 5–7 working days |
| Standard | ₹11,999 | Up to 10 pages; branded design; product/menu catalogue; enquiry sections; Search Console, Analytics, sitemap and Google Business Profile setup guidance | 8–14 working days |
| Premium | ₹24,999 | Up to 15 pages; advanced responsive UI; richer catalogue/filters; scoped integrations; technical SEO, Analytics, Google Business Profile optimization guidance, testing and handover | 15–25+ working days |

Domain, hosting, paid plugins, email services and third-party subscriptions are separate. Fixed packages do not include unlimited pages, complex e-commerce, SaaS, ERP/CRM, marketplace, AI systems or bespoke payment/login/booking workflows unless explicitly listed in an approved written scope. Google verification, indexing, ranking and business-profile approval are controlled by Google and are not guaranteed.

Custom website/web-platform projects outside these limits use a ₹999 requirements-review booking advance. It is not the full build price. A written scope, total quote, milestones and timeline must be approved before further payment or development.

## Payment and project start rules

1. Customer selects a clearly scoped fixed-price package and sees the complete payable amount before checkout.
2. Cashfree checkout uses the existing shared payment centre; payment is verified server-side before the application/brief form opens.
3. Customer must separately accept the existing Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy.
4. Fixed-price work starts after verified payment and a complete brief/content handover; stated timelines are estimates from that point.
5. Custom software/app/website enquiries pay only the clearly labelled ₹999 scope-review advance. This is not the full project price.
6. The full custom quote, milestones and schedule are shared for written customer approval before any further payment or development.
7. No balance is auto-charged. Domain, hosting, third-party costs and applicable taxes are disclosed clearly before commitment.
8. Sandbox/Preview payments are for testing only; no real customer payments are enabled by this document.

## Pricing review note

The starting prices are intentionally positioned as introductory, budget-conscious packages for small Indian businesses. Reassess delivery effort, revision volume, vendor costs, taxes and customer support before any public Production launch. Do not accept fixed-price checkout for work whose scope or cost is not defined.
